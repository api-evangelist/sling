# Morse

> Morse (morsemoney.com) is a money transfer app for sending money internationally. Fast, low-fee transfers to 150+ countries.

- https://morsemoney.com/fees

- https://morsemoney.com/legal

## Locales

The site is available in 10 languages: en, de, es, fr, hu, it, lt, pl, pt-BR, ro.
Localised pages are served under /{locale}/ prefixes (e.g. /de/fees, /fr/download).
The default locale (en) has no prefix.
